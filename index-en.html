Who We Are

We are Spectrum, a student team at Al-Balqa Applied University. We believe every student has talents and ideas worth sharing.

Our goal is to create an environment that brings students together and gives them opportunities to learn, share ideas, develop their skills, and collaborate on meaningful achievements.

At Spectrum, our differences are our strength, and every person brings a unique color to the bigger picture.

Our Vision

To become one of the leading student teams at Al-Balqa Applied University and build an inclusive student community that brings together talents and ideas from different faculties and academic disciplines.

We aim to give every student an opportunity to participate, grow, and make a meaningful impact on university life.

Spectrum aspires to become a space where students transform their potential into initiatives and achievements that leave a lasting mark both within and beyond the university.

Our Goals

Six goals guide everything we do — each one carrying its own color of the spectrum.

  1. 01. Build a Large Student Community

    Attract interested students and develop an active, collaborative community built on passion, initiative, and teamwork.

  2. 02. Reach Every Faculty Across the University

    Expand Spectrum's presence across faculties and disciplines at Al-Balqa Applied University, and open participation opportunities to students regardless of their major, in line with the team's current approved membership criteria.

  3. 03. Take Part in University Events

    Actively participate in university events, student activities, and initiatives, representing Spectrum in a positive and professional manner.

  4. 04. Launch Our Own Initiatives

    Develop and implement innovative ideas and initiatives created by Spectrum members, bringing students together and adding real value to university life.

  5. 05. Develop Members' Skills

    Provide opportunities for learning, hands-on experience, and knowledge sharing, and help members develop their communication, leadership, organization, and teamwork skills.

  6. 06. Build University Partnerships

    Collaborate with clubs, student teams, and related institutions to run joint projects and open new opportunities for students.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Is joining open to all students of Al-Balqa Applied University?

Welcome to Spectrum! Membership is currently available only to students of Al-Salt College at Al-Balqa Applied University.

2. Do I need previous experience to join?

Not necessarily. What matters most is being ready to learn, develop your skills, and work with the team. We welcome students with experience, and beginners also get the chance to learn and grow. Check the announced membership criteria for any application-specific requirements.

3. Is membership free?

Yes — joining Spectrum is completely free, and there are no membership fees.

4. How do I choose the right internal team for me?

Choose the area that matches your interests and the skills you want to develop. Review the available internal teams before filling in the registration form.

5. What happens after I submit the registration form?

After you submit the form, you go through an interview as part of the member selection process. Make sure your information is correct, and follow Spectrum's official channels for the latest updates.

6. When will acceptance results be announced?

The announcement date depends on official information released by Spectrum's management. Follow the official channels so you don't miss the announcement.

7. Where can I follow Spectrum's news?

Follow the team through the website and its official social media accounts, such as Instagram.

8. How can I contact the team's management?

You can reach the team's management through the official channels linked to the website and the team's social media accounts.
